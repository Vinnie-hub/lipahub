FAQ

Clear answers for you

How do I join?

Click “Get Started”, fill in your details and register. Then activate your account to unlock all earning activities.

Is there a fee?

Registration is free. A one-time activation fee unlocks all earning activities. It is set per country (for example KSH 550 in Kenya, USD 5 for other countries), and new members receive a 50% cashback welcome bonus.

How do I earn money?

Your main earnings come from partner opportunities: hotel reviews, chatting with foreigners and teaching Kiswahili. You can add to them by watching videos, answering trivia, writing articles, spinning for rewards and referring friends.

How do I withdraw?

Request a withdrawal from your account once your balance reaches the minimum, which is equal to your activation fee. You can be paid through M-Pesa, Airtel Money or MTN Mobile Money, depending on your country.

Where can I get help?

Use the live chat bubble at the bottom of the page, or email LipaHub Support.
